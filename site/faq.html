ancora FAQ

Can ancora get me banned in VRChat?

ancora never touches VRChat or its files. It is a SteamVR driver that changes the stick values your controller driver reports, inside SteamVR's own process. A game only sees ordinary stick values. Nobody can promise anything about VRChat's rules, but ancora does not modify the game.

What is Thumb Gate?

Thumb Gate is ancora's main fix for Meta Quest thumbstick drift. Quest sticks have a thumb sensor. With your thumb off the stick, games get stick centered, so drift cannot move you. With your thumb on, the stick works as normal.

Does ancora slow anything down?

No. The driver does a few calculations per stick update while SteamVR runs. The panel uses a few MB of memory and draws nothing while hidden in the tray.

Does ancora change my buttons or other controller settings?

No. Only the thumbstick, and only when needed. Buttons, triggers, grip, haptics and SteamVR bindings are untouched.

Which headsets and connections work?

Quest controllers (Touch style sticks with a thumb sensor) on Windows 10 or 11. Tested with Steam Link. Virtual Desktop, ALVR and Quest Link also hand controllers to SteamVR through a driver, so they should work the same way, but they are not tested yet.

How do I remove ancora?

Close SteamVR, then uninstall ancora from Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps. This removes everything it added, including its SteamVR entries.
