Why your VRChat character moves by itself

Your avatar slides forward or spins slowly while your hands rest. This is almost always thumbstick drift, not a VRChat bug.

What stick drift is

A thumbstick has two small sensors that measure how far it is pushed. With wear, dust or a weak spring, the stick rests slightly off center and reports a small push that nobody made. Games read that as input, so the character moves.

Why it shows up in VRChat

VRChat moves you continuously with the stick, so even a tiny value moves you for as long as it lasts. The result is often a slow walk or turn you cannot stop.

Fixes people try, and their limits

  • Clean and recalibrate: Meta suggests recalibrating thumbstick range and deadzone in headset settings. Helps light cases.
  • Bigger deadzone: hides drift but makes slow, careful movement impossible.
  • Replace the controller: works, but costs money and wear returns.

How ancora fixes it

Quest sticks have a capacitive thumb sensor. ancora is a SteamVR driver that watches it: thumb off the stick means games get "centered", so drift cannot reach the game. Thumb on the stick means normal input. If the sensor misses your thumb, holding the stick at its edge for a quarter second makes ancora stop trusting the sensor until it sees your thumb again.

It never touches VRChat or its files. See how it works.
