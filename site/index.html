Meta Quest thumbstick drift fix for SteamVR and VRChat

Is your VRChat character walking or turning on its own when you are not touching the stick? That is thumbstick drift. ancora is a free SteamVR driver that fixes Meta Quest thumbstick drift with Thumb Gate: when your thumb is off the stick, games get "stick centered". When your thumb is on the stick, everything works as normal.

Download ancora for Windows How to install

  • Quest ready. Works with Quest 3, Quest 2 and Quest Pro (Touch style sticks with a thumb sensor).
  • Game files untouched. It runs inside SteamVR only, never inside VRChat.
  • Free and small. No admin rights, and you can turn it off or remove it at any time.

Why Thumb Gate beats recalibrating or adding a deadzone

A big deadzone makes slow walking impossible, and recalibration does not help a stick that rests off center. ancora uses the capacitive thumb sensor: no thumb on the stick means the stick cannot be pushed, so drift is blocked without losing precision. Read why stick drift happens in VRChat.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
  • SteamVR installed and started once.
  • A Quest connected to SteamVR. Tested with Steam Link; Virtual Desktop, ALVR and Quest Link should work the same way but are not tested yet.

Install in four steps

  1. Close SteamVR.
  2. Download the newest ancora-setup installer from Releases.
  3. Run it and click through.
  4. Start SteamVR. An anchor icon appears in the tray. Done.

Frequently asked questions

Can ancora get me banned in VRChat?

ancora never touches VRChat or its files. It is a SteamVR driver that changes the stick values your controller driver reports, inside SteamVR's own process. A game only sees ordinary stick values. Nobody can promise anything about VRChat's rules, but ancora does not modify the game.

What is Thumb Gate?

Thumb Gate is ancora's main fix for Meta Quest thumbstick drift. Quest sticks have a thumb sensor. With your thumb off the stick, games get stick centered, so drift cannot move you. With your thumb on, the stick works as normal.

Does ancora slow anything down?

No. The driver does a few calculations per stick update while SteamVR runs. The panel uses a few MB of memory and draws nothing while hidden in the tray.
