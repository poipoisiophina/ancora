How ancora works

ancora is a SteamVR driver. It sits between your controller driver and the game and only changes the thumbstick.

Thumb Gate

Thumb off the stick: games get "stick centered". Thumb on: input passes through. Keep it on; it is the main fix. Read the Thumb Gate guide.

Optional tools

  • Deadzone: only if the stick drifts while your thumb rests on it. Leave off otherwise.
  • Tune page: measures your stick and suggests a deadzone, re-centers a stick that rests off to one side, and stretches directions where a worn stick stops short of full.
  • Smoothing and curve: for a shaky stick or finer control near the middle. They do not fix drift.

Safe by design

Nothing touches the game process or its files. Everything runs inside SteamVR. Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ancora\ancora.ini and save by themselves with no restart. The panel speaks many languages, including every official EU language.
